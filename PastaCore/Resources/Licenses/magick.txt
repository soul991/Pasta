Before we can address the questions of the terms and conditions of an ImageMagick copyright, we must first address what the license doesn't say. The ImageMagick license gives you permission to display, copy, edit, redistribute and incorporate ImageMagick into other software products without paying a fee. It does not require you to provide source code for a work you distribute that incorporates ImageMagick. It also does not require you to distribute your work under the same terms as ImageMagick.

Terms and Conditions for Use, Reproduction, and Distribution
