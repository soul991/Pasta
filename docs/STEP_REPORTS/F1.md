## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

An audit of the repository identified major document contradictions across Stage 1:
- `docs/LICENSE_AUDIT.md` previously claimed "DO NOT BUNDLE" for all engines and claimed that Pasta ships zero engines.
- `README.md`, `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`, `SECURITY.md`, and `CONTRIBUTING.md` instructed users to `brew install` engines and claimed engines were not bundled.
- `docs/ENGINES.md` was hand-edited and not tied mechanically to the canonical engine manifest.

Step F1 corrects these contradictions to make all documents accurately reflect the bundled reality:
1. Rewrote `docs/LICENSE_AUDIT.md`: All 19 tools registered in `ToolLocator` and present in `Engines/engines.json` are marked **Bundled** (with exact packaging types), obligations (written source offers, license bundling, isolated subprocess execution) and risk levels updated. Removed all contradictory "DO NOT BUNDLE" statements.
2. Created `scripts/generate_engines_doc.py` to mechanically generate `docs/ENGINES.md` from `Engines/engines.json` (and `engines.lock.json`), with `--check` flag wired into both CI jobs in `.github/workflows/macos-ci.yml`.
3. Updated `README.md`, `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`, `SECURITY.md`, and `CONTRIBUTING.md` to state that all 19 engines are bundled within `Pasta.app/Contents/Resources/Engines/`, with Homebrew instructions reserved strictly for optional developer builds.
4. Updated Section 41 tests in `PastaTests/main.swift` to enforce that tools whose source in `engines.json` is bundled must not be described as "Do Not Bundle" in `LICENSE_AUDIT.md`.
