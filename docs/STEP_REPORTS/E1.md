## 4. Objectives & Implementation Summary

### 4.1 Engine Inventory & Relocatable Bundling
Every tool in `ToolLocator` is bundled into `Pasta.app/Contents/Resources/Engines/` with:
- **Zero external paths**: All transitive dynamic libraries (88 dylibs, ~77 MB) copied to `Engines/lib/` and load commands rewritten via `install_name_tool` to `@loader_path/../lib` (from `bin/`) and `@loader_path/` (from `lib/`). Homebrew `LC_RPATH` commands stripped.
- **ImageMagick Relocatability**: Copied `modules-Q16HDRI/coders` to `Engines/lib/ImageMagick/modules-Q16HDRI/coders/`, wiped hardcoded `/opt/homebrew` `dependency_libs` and `libdir` from `.la` files, sanitized `configure.xml`, rewritten coder dylib links to `@loader_path/../../..`, signed modules, and wrapped `bin/magick` to configure `MAGICK_HOME`, `MAGICK_CODER_MODULE_PATH`, and `MAGICK_CONFIGURE_PATH`.
- **Ghostscript Support**: Bundled `gs-bin` Mach-O with wrapper `bin/gs` configuring `GS_LIB` pointing to bundled Ghostscript resource files.
- **Perl Runtime**: Relocated `exiftool` with bundled `@INC` pointing to sibling `lib/perl5/`.
- **System Tool Wrappers**: Replaced symbolic links for `sips`, `ditto`, and `tar` with executable wrapper scripts (`exec /usr/bin/<tool> "$@"`) to strictly comply with Apple's bundle validation rules (`codesign --strict`).
- **Hardened Runtime & Entitlements**: Signed every Mach-O binary and dylib with `--options runtime` and `assets/entitlements.plist` containing `com.apple.security.cs.disable-library-validation`.
- **Clean Environment Launch Test**: Verified every bundled engine launches from the bundle under an isolated `PATH=/usr/bin:/bin` environment and reports its version.
